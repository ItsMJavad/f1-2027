Javad Edges Hamilton in Baku Thriller as Both McLarens Crash Out

Baku GP, Azerbaijan - Round 13 of the 2027 Formula One World Championship

In a dramatic afternoon on the streets of Baku, Javad claimed victory after a tense, race-long duel with Lewis Hamilton, with the two trading lap times and pressure all the way to the chequered flag. The Ferrari driver held firm under relentless pursuit, taking one of his hardest-earned wins of the season.

The race began with Hamilton leading from pole, but Javad wasted no time attacking down the long main straight. From lap 1 to lap 40, neither driver pulled more than two seconds over the other, producing one of the most intense fights of the 2027 season. Hamilton ultimately finished +0.792s back, a razor-thin margin after nearly 18 minutes of racing.

Behind them, Max Verstappen took P3 but finished a distant +16.796 off the lead after lacking the pace of the front two. George Russell brought home P4, continuing Mercedes' strong weekend.

Ferrari's Charles Leclerc ended P5 after a clean but quiet race, while Yuki Tsunoda, who was running a strong P4 early on, fell backward after contact from Lando Norris forced him into an extra pit stop. The Red Bull driver recovered to P6 after the delay.

Baku was disastrous for McLaren. On lap 2, Oscar Piastri clipped the inside wall, spinning and crashing out instantly. On lap 8, a collision between Norris and Tsunoda sent Norris into the barriers—ending McLaren's day with a double DNF.

Sauber had a decent afternoon, with Hülkenberg P7 and Bortoleto P8, both keeping out of trouble in a chaotic midfield.
