Austrian Masterclass: Javad Shines at the Red Bull Ring as Hamilton Delivers a Stunning Drive

Red Bull Ring - Round 7 of the 2027 Formula One World Championship

The Styrian hills echoed with excitement as Ferrari's Javad delivered yet another brilliant victory, overcoming both Red Bulls and his own teammate to claim his sixth win of the season. The Red Bull Ring produced chaos, comebacks, and wheel-to-wheel combat — and Javad once again rose above the rest.

A Start Full of Fire

Max Verstappen started from pole, but his hopes of a home-track triumph evaporated almost immediately. Javad quickly overtook Leclerc for P2 on lap 1, and just one lap later, the Ferrari driver blasted past Verstappen to take the lead. From that moment on, he controlled the race flawlessly.

While Javad disappeared into the distance, the real drama unfolded behind him.

Hamilton's Drive of the Season

Lewis Hamilton produced one of the most impressive performances of 2027 so far. Starting fourth, the Mercedes star displayed relentless pace, clean overtakes, and remarkable consistency on a circuit known for punishing laps.

His defining moment came on lap 7, when he fought Verstappen for P2. The two made contact in Turn 3 — Hamilton continued without damage while Verstappen ran wide and dropped to P8. From there, Hamilton kept pushing, carving through the field and securing a phenomenal second place, finishing just 10.866 seconds behind the leader.

Ferrari Double Podium

Charles Leclerc completed the podium with a steady drive to third, fending off Oscar Piastri as the McLaren driver mounted late pressure but ultimately finished P4.

Midfield Battles and Strong Finishes

Sauber once again impressed:

Hülkenberg - P5, another consistent and clean performance Bortoleto - P7, staying in the points and strengthening his rookie campaign

Lando Norris secured P6, while Verstappen could only recover to P8 after the lap 7 incident. Russell brought the second Mercedes home in P9, and Tsunoda closed the order in P10.
