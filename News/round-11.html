Javad Dominates Montreal as Leclerc Delivers a Stunning Comeback

Circuit Gilles Villeneuve, Canada - Round 11 of the 2027 Formula One World Championship

Montreal served up a cool, dry Canadian Grand Prix — but the action on track was anything but. From pole position, Javad delivered a flawless lights-to-flag victory, never losing control of the race for even a moment. The Ferrari driver was in a league of his own, setting the fastest lap and crossing the line 42.935 seconds ahead of the field.

Behind him, Charles Leclerc produced one of the drives of the season. Despite qualifying only P5, the Monegasque carved through the pack with precision and consistency, eventually climbing to P2, securing Ferrari a dominant 1-2 finish. His race pace was unmatched by anyone except Javad.

Lewis Hamilton put on a strong showing of his own, fending off early pressure and keeping Verstappen behind to secure P3, returning Mercedes to the podium.

Meanwhile, Max Verstappen struggled with balance issues throughout the race. Although he began P3, he slipped to P4, unable to match the front-running pace.

McLaren had a mixed day — Lando Norris finished a solid P5, while Oscar Piastri slid back from his strong qualifying position to P9 after a difficult middle stint.

Sauber enjoyed double points once again: Hülkenberg finished P7, with Bortoleto close behind in P8, both capitalizing on Tsunoda's lack of pace and Piastri's struggles.

Red Bull's second car, Yuki Tsunoda, had a lonely race, ending the day in P10, last of the finishers.

Ferrari leave Canada with maximum momentum — and a championship leader extending his advantage yet again.
