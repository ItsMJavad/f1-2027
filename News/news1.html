Javad's Stunning Comeback Victory Caps Off a Dramatic Desert Finale

Abu Dhabi, UAE - Round 16 of the 2027 Formula One World Championship

Under the floodlights of Yas Marina, Round 16 delivered one of the most dramatic races of the season. A disastrous start for Javad, early chaos with two DNFs, championship twists, and a tense three-way battle at the front left fans breathless until the final lap.

🌟 Javad's Incredible Recovery Drive - From P8 on Lap 1 to Victory

Starting P4, Javad was immediately swallowed by the pack after a poor launch, falling all the way down to P8 by the end of the opening lap. But from that moment on, he unleashed one of the finest drives of the season.

Lap after lap, he carved through the field:

* Passing Piastri with a brave dive into Turn 9,
* Outsmarting Tsunoda with a perfectly timed switchback,
* Closing down two seconds per lap on the leaders during the mid-race phase.

By the final stint, he was the fastest man on track by a huge margin — and with an assertive overtake on Leclerc, he completed a masterclass comeback victory, sealing yet another win for Ferrari.

He also set the fastest lap, putting an exclamation mark on a heroic performance.

🔴 Ferrari Strong — Leclerc Fights Hard, Holds Off Hamilton

Charles Leclerc, starting from pole, looked confident in the opening laps. He held the lead early, and despite Javad's late charge, he managed to stay ahead of Hamilton, finishing a strong P2.

Lewis Hamilton had a superb start, jumping from P5 to P2 on the first lap with a bold move around the outside of Turn 5. But small mistakes — running wide twice in Sector 3 — cost him the chance to challenge for the win. He ultimately settled for P3, but his pace was strong enough to earn him the title of most consistent driver of the race.

⬇️ Verstappen's Downward Spiral

Starting P2, expectations were high for Max Verstappen — but the race unraveled quickly.

A snap of oversteer on cold tyres cost him two positions in Lap 1. Later, brake temperature issues forced him to slow just enough to lose further ground. He eventually finished in P6, far from the podium fight and visibly frustrated.

💥 Early Chaos - Bortoleto & Norris Out

Two major incidents shaped the opening phase of the race:

Lap 1 - Gabriel Bortoleto DNF

Bortoleto's race ended almost immediately after lights out. While fighting with Hulkenberg into Turn 3, he clipped the inside kerb too hard. The Sauber snapped sideways and slammed into the outside wall — a heavy impact but thankfully with no injuries. A worst-case scenario for the young Brazilian, who had been targeting points.

Lap 3 - Lando Norris DNF

Two laps later, Norris' race ended in equally heartbreaking fashion. Locked in a tight battle with Piastri, he went slightly too deep into Turn 11. Under heavy braking, the McLaren locked its front-right wheel, sending Norris straight into the barriers. His frustration on the radio said it all — a costly unforced error after a competitive qualifying.

Both incidents brought out short yellow flags but avoided a full safety car.
