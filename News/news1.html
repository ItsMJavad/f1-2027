Hamilton Holds Off Javad in Spa Classic as Russell's Late Problem Drops Him to Last

Spa, Belgium - Round 14 of the 2027 Formula One World Championship

The sweeping forests of Spa-Francorchamps delivered another dramatic chapter of the 2027 season as Lewis Hamilton claimed victory after a fiercely fought battle with Javad, who crossed the line a mere 0.262 seconds behind. The gap between the two remained under a second for most of the final laps, but even with superior pace in sector two, Javad could not find a way past the seven-time world champion.

The race began in chaos for pole-sitter Max Verstappen, who made multiple small errors on the opening lap, losing the lead and slipping behind both Hamilton and Javad almost immediately. Although he recovered well, the early mistakes cost him any chance of challenging for the win.

Further back, Yuki Tsunoda and Oscar Piastri delivered excellent performances, climbing from the back rows and showing pace strong enough to challenge the midfield leaders. Their consistency earned them valuable points at a circuit where confidence and rhythm are everything.

One of the biggest heartbreaks of the afternoon came for George Russell. Running a solid P4, he suffered a mechanical issue in the final 10 laps—losing power through the long uphill Kemmel straight. The problem worsened, and he slid all the way down to P10, just barely finishing the race.

Ferrari's Charles Leclerc had a clean but quiet run in P4, while McLaren's Lando Norris rounded out the top five after a strong mid-race charge.
